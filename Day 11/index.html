The University of Vavuniya is a public state university in Sri Lanka.
Established in August 2021, it is the country's 17th national university,
located on a 195-acre residential campus in Pampaimadu, approximately
10 kilometers from Vavuniya town along the Vavuniya-Mannar Road.
